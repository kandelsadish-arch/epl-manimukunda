Week 1 Activity Report
August 5, 2026

Community Clean-up Campaign

🔍 View
🔍 View
🔍 View
🔍 View

The Environment Protection League (EPL MMC) officially kicked off its monthly environmental initiative with an inspiring Community Clean-up Campaign at Green River Park. Over 80 passionate volunteers gathered early Saturday morning to restore our public recreational spaces.

Our team organized systematically into four specialized squads covering park trails, riverbanks, children’s play zones, and perimeter walkways. Equipped with biodegradable bags, protective gloves, and safety grabbers, volunteers collected over 350 kilograms of solid waste, including single-use plastic bottles, aluminum cans, food packaging, and non-biodegradable debris.

All gathered materials were brought to our mobile sorting station where 65% of the waste was successfully separated and dispatched to local municipal recycling facilities. Local park authorities praised EPL MMC for mobilizing youth and family participation in maintaining pristine natural habitats.

Week 2 Activity Report
August 12, 2026

Tree Planting & Reforestation Drive

🔍 View
🔍 View
🔍 View
🔍 View

For Week 2, EPL MMC organized a major Reforestation Drive at the North Ridge Ecological Reserve. In partnership with local forestry experts, we planted 250 native saplings to restore degraded hillside slopes and fight soil erosion.

Volunteers planted native species including Oak, Pine, Maple, and flowering dogwood trees specially chosen for their climate resilience and ability to nurture indigenous bird species. Each tree was tagged with a digital tracking label so EPL MMC youth monitors can record growth and soil moisture levels over the coming seasons.

Special workshops were conducted alongside the planting session to educate participants on proper soil enrichment techniques, organic composting, and root watering strategies. This ongoing effort serves as a vital step towards increasing our regional urban forest canopy.

Week 3 Activity Report
August 19, 2026

Plastic Pollution Awareness & Recycling Drive

🔍 View
🔍 View
🔍 View

Week 3 focused on combating plastic pollution through education, active collection, and circular economic solutions. EPL MMC set up an interactive pop-up recycling hub in the central city square.

Over 200 households participated by bringing clean plastic bottles, containers, and electronics for recycling. In exchange, EPL MMC distributed reusable cotton shopping bags, glass water bottles, and home composting starter kits.

Our educators conducted interactive demonstrations highlighting the life cycle of single-use plastics and practical ways to transition to zero-waste households. More than 150 citizens signed our 'Zero Single-Use Plastic' pledge to reduce personal waste footprints.

Week 4 Activity Report

Renewable Energy & Water Conservation Workshop

To cap off our monthly series, EPL MMC hosted a community workshop on Renewable Energy Systems and Urban Water Conservation at the Green City Innovation Hall.

Energy engineers demonstrated accessible rooftop solar micro-installations, solar water heaters, and energy-efficient LED upgrades. Participants learned how household solar adoption significantly lowers carbon emissions while decreasing utility costs.

The water conservation segment showcased low-cost rainwater harvesting systems, greywater recycling filters, and drip irrigation techniques for urban gardens. Over 60 community leaders committed to installing rain barrels in their gardens prior to the upcoming autumn rain season.

Our Mission

About Environment Protection League

EPL MMC is a grassroots environmental organization dedicated to promoting ecological awareness, organizing direct action campaigns, and protecting nature for future generations.

Empowering Communities for Environmental Stewardship

Founded with a passion for environmental justice and habitat preservation, the Environment Protection League brings together students, professionals, scientists, and local citizens to build sustainable ecosystems.

We believe that true environmental protection starts with consistent, local action. From weekly cleanup drives to policy advocacy, our team works tirelessly to create measurable impact in our environment.

🌱 Conservation

Restoring native habitats, planting trees, and protecting biodiversity.

♻️ Zero Waste

Educating youth and communities in eco-friendly living.
